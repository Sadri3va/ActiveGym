Getting in

Three seconds and you're in.

  1. Open the appYour QR code is in the "Účet" (Account) section, top left.
  2. Hold it to the readerThe reader is right next to the entrance door.
  3. Walk in and trainThe door opens. Entry is possible daily from 5:00 to 20:30.
