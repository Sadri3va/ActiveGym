For new clients

How it works

Registration is only possible in person. It matters for safety — since there is no reception, we want to meet everyone who trains with us.

01

Message or call us

Contact us on WhatsApp — by message or call.

  • WhatsApp: +421 908 376 297
02

We arrange a meeting

We'll find a time that suits you. The meeting takes place at the gym and only takes a few minutes.

03

Registration on site

At the meeting we register you, show you around and help you set up the app.

04

Walk in with your QR code

You get a personal QR code in the app. Hold it to the reader at the entrance and start training! Future renewals you handle yourself in the app.

What to bring

Payment

To pay your first membership — cash or card.

Self-service gym

A few rules for everyone

There is no reception, so we rely on every member to be considerate.

  • Enter only with your own QR code
  • Don't let anyone else in
  • Put equipment back after use
  • Leave the gym by 22:00
  • Bring clean indoor shoes

The premises are monitored by CCTV.
