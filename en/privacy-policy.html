2. What data we process and why

We process personal data only to the extent necessary for each purpose.

2.1 Contact via WhatsApp and phone

Data
name, phone number, content of messages and calls
Purpose
arranging a personal meeting, answering questions
Legal basis
steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Retention
6 months after the last contact if you do not register

2.2 Registration and membership

Data
full name, e-mail
Purpose
concluding and performing the membership contract, verifying the member's identity
Legal basis
performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Retention
for the duration of the membership and 3 years after it ends

2.3 App and gym entry

Data
user account, personal QR code, entry records (date and time), membership validity
Purpose
enabling entry to the gym, managing and renewing membership
Legal basis
performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Retention
for the duration of the membership / entry records 12 months

2.4 Payments and accounting

Data
billing details, payment records
Purpose
bookkeeping and tax obligations
Legal basis
legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), Slovak Accounting Act
Retention
10 years from the end of the year the records relate to

2.5 CCTV

Data
video recordings of people on the gym premises
Purpose
protection of property, safety of members and prevention of entry misuse
Legal basis
legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
Retention
72 hours, longer only when dealing with a specific incident

2.6 Website operation

Data
IP address, browser type, time of visit (technical server logs)
Purpose
ensuring the operation and security of the website
Legal basis
legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
Retention
for as long as necessary to ensure the security of the site, according to the policies of the hosting provider GitHub, Inc.

3. Legitimate interest

For CCTV and website operation we process data on the basis of legitimate interest. Our legitimate interest is protecting property and the safety of people in a self-service gym without permanent staff, and keeping the website secure and working. You may object to this processing at any time (see section 7).

4. Who we share data with

Your data may also be processed by the following parties who provide services to us (processors) or to whom we disclose it:

  • Clubspire s.r.o. — provider of the app and entry system
  • GitHub, Inc. — website hosting (GitHub Pages)
  • Meta — if you contact us via WhatsApp
  • Accounting office — bookkeeping
  • GoPay Czech — processing card payments in the app
  • Public authorities where required by law (e.g. the police during an investigation)

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers (GitHub, Meta/WhatsApp) may also process data in the USA. Transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework / standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

6. How long we keep data

We keep data only for the periods stated for each purpose in section 2. After that, we delete or anonymise it unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. Your rights

In connection with the processing of your personal data, you have the right to:

  • access your data and obtain a copy,
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected,
  • have your data erased if it is no longer needed or processed unlawfully,
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability for data you have provided to us,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

You can exercise your rights by e-mail at albina.masarykova@gmail.com. We will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

8. Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe the processing of your data breaches the law, you can lodge a complaint with the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic, Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, www.dataprotection.gov.sk.

9. Obligation to provide data

Providing the data needed for registration is a contractual requirement. Without it, we cannot conclude a membership or give you access to the gym.

10. Automated decision-making

We do not carry out automated decision-making or profiling with legal effects on you.

11. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or any analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are stored on our own website and the site does not load content from third parties.

12. Changes to this document

We may update this document, for example when laws or our services change. The current version is always published on this page together with its effective date.
